Mood Notes

  • 💙

    Sadness is not weakness—it's a natural human emotion that signals you care deeply about something or someone.

  • 💧

    Allow yourself to feel without judgment. Suppressing emotions often makes them heavier; acknowledging them creates space for healing.

  • 🤝

    Reach out to someone you trust if you need support. Connection, even in small doses, can lighten the weight you're carrying.

  • 🌱

    Be gentle with yourself. You don't have to "fix" everything right now. Sometimes healing starts with simply being present with what is.

  • ⏳

    Remember that this feeling, like all feelings, is temporary. You've moved through difficult moments before, and you will again.
